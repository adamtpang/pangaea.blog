# The 1,000 songs

## The lego model (all five pillars share one shape)

Every piece of work is a STACK, not a single artifact. The reason 1,000 feels
impossible is that you picture the top of the stack every time. You do not have
to. A piece can exist at any layer, and a lower layer is not a failure, it is
an entry.

| Layer | Essay | Song | App |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 · the claim | Title (a claim, not a topic) | Title + the hook | Name + domain |
| 1 · the skeleton | Outline | **Key, tempo, chords** | One-line thesis |
| 2 · the voice | Draft prose | **Melody + lyrics** | Landing page |
| 3 · the artifact | 300 words | **Demo recording** | Working MVP |
| 4 · the polish | Edited, interlinked | **Produced (Ableton)** | Live product |
| 5 · the release | `draft: false` | SoundCloud / Spotify | Public URL |

**The floor, per pillar. Never zero:**

- Essay: 300 words. Or just a stub with a real claim.
- **Song: a 30-second voice memo of a chord progression with a hummed melody.**
- App: a domain and one sentence saying what it does.

You are already doing this. `voice note #1` through `#5` are published on your
SoundCloud right now. Those ARE song seedlings. They counted the whole time.

## The four stages

Every song entry carries `stage:`. Only `released` implies a public link.

- **sketch** · a title, maybe chords, maybe a hummed idea. The floor.
- **demo** · a real recording exists, rough. Phone quality is fine.
- **produced** · arranged and mixed in Ableton.
- **released** · public on SoundCloud, Spotify, or YouTube.

The counter on `/work` should light for ALL of them. A thousand songs means a
thousand *entries*, not a thousand masters. Visakanv's 1,000 works because the
unit is small.

## Your instrument stack

Drums, bass, guitar, some piano, vocals, production. That is a whole band in one
person, which means **no song is ever blocked on a collaborator.** Put what you
actually played in `instruments:` so the catalog shows the range.

## The daily floor for songs

One of these, every day. Any one counts:

1. Pick up a guitar, find a progression, record 30 seconds into your phone.
2. Open an existing sketch and add ONE layer (put a bassline on it).
3. Write one verse of lyrics into a song file.
4. Fill in `key:` and `tempo:` and `chords:` on a sketch you never documented.

Rule: touch the pile daily, thicken one entry. Do not start from zero and do
not aim for a master.

## The obsessions

Music, philosophy, and business. The songs pillar and the essays pillar are the
same worldview in two mediums, so let them cross-link. An essay about ambition
burning dirty should point at a song that sounds like it. That interlinking is
the thing nobody else can copy, because nobody else has both catalogs.
