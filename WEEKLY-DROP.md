# The weekly drop

`RITUAL.md` already says ship one real piece every Friday. It has been
honored 5 times since April. This file exists because the rule was never
the problem: **the choice was.**

Every Friday started as an open decision across hundreds of options, and an
open decision at the moment of maximum Resistance is how nothing ships.
So the choice moves off Adam and onto the agent, and it gets locked.

## The rule

1. Claude picks ONE essay from the pool. Adam does not pick.
2. That pick is the **only** thing he is allowed to write toward for the
   week. Not a suggestion, a lane.
3. It ships by Friday, `draft: false`, live.
4. Adam holds one veto. Using it means Claude picks again immediately, same
   week, no renegotiating the cadence.
5. Miss once and it carries to next Friday alongside the new pick. Never
   miss twice, inherited straight from `RITUAL.md`.

## The reframe that makes this sustainable

53 essays are already drafted with real bodies, sitting at `draft: true`.
387 more are empty stubs. Published: 5.

So the weekly quota is **a finishing quota, not a writing quota.** There is
already a full year of Fridays written. The work each week is verify,
polish, ship, not generate from a blank page. That is why weekly is
achievable now when it was not before.

Pool: the 53 real drafts. Empty stubs are not eligible; a stub is not an
S-tier candidate, it is a title.

## The S-tier bar

Split honestly, because two of these are not Claude's call.

**What Claude can judge from the repo:**
- The title is a claim, not a topic.
- The body is real and honest length, not padded to hit a number.
- It is grounded in something checkable: Adam's own life, or a real cited
  source. Nothing invented.
- There is a line worth pulling as a quote.
- Leverage: `video_ready`, or it already carries cover and song, so one
  week's work can feed the reel pipeline too.
- No open blocker. An essay needing external fact-verification is not
  shippable in a 5-day window unless the verification IS the week's work.

**What only Adam can judge:**
- Whether he still believes it.
- Whether he has something real left to say there this week.

Those two are the veto's whole job.
