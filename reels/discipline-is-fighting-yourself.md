## Hook (0-3s)
On-screen text: "Discipline is a lie productive people tell you."
VO: You think disciplined people are just better at forcing themselves through work they hate. They're not.
Visual: talking head, direct to camera, static.

## Beat 1: the misconception (3-10s)
On-screen text: "Discipline" = forcing yourself through it
VO: We watch someone grind and call it discipline: the willpower to push through unpleasant work. That's the story. It's wrong.
Visual: quick cut to a stock "grinding at a desk" clip, slightly ironic.

## Beat 2: what's actually happening (10-20s)
On-screen text: They're not fighting themselves. They're not conflicted.
VO: When it actually works, it's not force. It's that they've stopped being conflicted about what to do. No internal argument, no need to override yourself.
Visual: back to talking head.

## Beat 3: the cost (20-30s)
On-screen text: Discipline = wasted energy fighting yourself
VO: Every bit of willpower you spend forcing yourself is energy not spent solving the actual problem, the one causing the resistance in the first place.
Visual: simple text card, no b-roll, let it land.

## Beat 4: the real move (30-40s)
On-screen text: Ask why part of you doesn't want to.
VO: Instead of forcing it, get curious about the resistance. There's a reason part of you doesn't want to do the thing. Find it, and the fight disappears. Once you're unconflicted, hard work stops feeling hard.
Visual: talking head.
