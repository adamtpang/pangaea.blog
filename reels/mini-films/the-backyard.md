## Cold open (0:00-0:45)

VISUAL: Photo-style shot, a backyard under a bright blue sky, out of focus. Hold.

VO: About ten years after my parents divorced, in a backyard, under a bright blue sky, they hugged me. Both of them. At the same time.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "That is the entire event. A hug in a backyard."
[Source: The Backyard]

VO: I'm going to tell you what it took to get there.

VISUAL: Title card. "THE BACKYARD"

## Part 1: The island (0:45-2:00)

VISUAL: A map-style graphic, a small island in a large blue field.

VO: Two hundred million years ago there was one continent. Every piece of land on earth, touching. Then it broke, and the pieces drifted, and now there are islands.

VO: I was born on one of them.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Guam is thirty miles long. You can drive its whole length in an afternoon."
[Source: Pangaea]

VO: From the air it's a green comma in an enormous blue nothing, closer to Tokyo than to any part of the country that owns it.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "The world is real but it is happening somewhere else. Everything arrives late."
[Source: Pangaea]

VO: Movies, products, news, weather that already hit the Philippines. You learn the shape of being downstream.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "I grew up watching a bigger world arrive on a delay and quietly deciding I would rather be where it started."
[Source: Pangaea]

## Part 2: The break (2:00-3:30)

VISUAL: Two small houses, a path between them.

VO: My parents divorced when I was ten.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "For ten years after, family was a logistics problem. Two houses. Two calendars. A kid with a backpack, moving between them."
[Source: The Backyard / Pangaea]

VO: When you're ten, your family isn't a relationship. It's a place. It's the ground.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "When it comes apart, what you actually lose is not the people. You keep the people. You lose the ground."
[Source: Pangaea]

VO: What you get instead is a skill.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "The ability to be fine in two places and fully at home in neither."
[Source: Pangaea]

VO: That skill is genuinely useful. I've used it constantly. It's also the reason I've spent most of my adult life building rooms for other people to stand in.

## Part 3: Making things that hold people (3:30-5:30)

VISUAL: A stage, a small crowd, silhouettes.

VO: I finished high school in 2020 and started writing music seriously.

VO: Not as a career plan. A song is the smallest possible thing that can hold more than one person at a time.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "You make it alone in a room and then it exists somewhere between you and whoever hears it."
[Source: Pangaea]

VO: For someone who'd spent a decade shuttling between two houses, a place that could contain several people at once, and didn't require anybody to agree on anything, was a very appealing kind of object.

VO: Then in 2022 I graduated App Academy and became a software engineer. The same instinct, with better tooling.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "An app is a room. You build it alone and then strangers stand in it."
[Source: Pangaea]

VO: I didn't understand these were the same activity until much later.

## Part 4: Arriving on day one (5:30-6:45)

VISUAL: Timeline graphic, matching Early to Big Things' visual language.

VO: I was on ChatGPT day one. Network School, day one.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Being early is what being downstream turns into if you get angry about it early enough."
[Source: Pangaea]

VO: I grew up on a delay. So now when something new appears and a quiet part of me says huh, I go touch it that day. Not because I'm good at predicting the future. Because I spent my childhood on the receiving end of it, and I'd rather not do that again.

## Part 5: The island I picked (6:45-8:00)

VISUAL: Photo-style shot, a founder community, tables and laptops.

VO: In 2025 I moved to Network School full-time and became longtermer number two.

VO: The joke I didn't notice for almost a year is that I left an island to live on an island.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Langkawi is also thirty-something miles across. From the air, honestly, it looks like home."
[Source: Pangaea]

VO: The difference isn't the geography. This one is full on purpose.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Five hundred founders came here to be in the same place at the same time."
[Source: Pangaea]

VO: That's the whole difference between being stranded and being gathered.

## Part 6: What I've actually been building (8:00-9:15)

VISUAL: A grid of small labeled boxes, matching the hand-drawn project grid from Early to Big Things.

VO: I ship a lot of small things, and for years I couldn't explain the pattern. Laid out now, it isn't subtle.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Wonderhall is a concert series. A room, on a schedule, where people are in the same place hearing the same thing."
[Source: Pangaea]

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Summon is a way to talk to the people who shaped you. Rockefeller, Deutsch, Sivers, Naval."
[Source: Pangaea]

VO: Optimism is a map of what humanity wants. Pangaea is a blog named after the moment before the land broke.

VO: I didn't plan any of that. I just kept building the same object, over and over, with different materials.

## Part 7: The backyard, in full (9:15-9:50)

VISUAL: Return to the backyard photo from the cold open, now held in full frame.

VO: About ten years after the divorce, in a backyard, under a bright blue sky, my parents hugged me. Both of them. The three of us standing together.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "It fixed nothing, undid nothing, and changed no arrangement. But for the length of it, everything that had come apart was back together."
[Source: The Backyard]

VO: Continents don't reassemble. Pangaea broke, and it isn't coming back. The pieces are still drifting apart right now, about as fast as your fingernails grow.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "That is real and it is not sad. What happened instead is that things grew on the pieces."
[Source: Pangaea]

## Outro (9:50-10:15)

VISUAL: End card, plain text on paper background.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "I was born on an island. I live on one now. In between I learned that the only real cure for scattered is to build somewhere worth gathering."
[Source: Pangaea]

VO: That's the name. That's the whole story.
