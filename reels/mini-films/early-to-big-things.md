## Cold open (0:00-0:40)

VISUAL: Screen recording style, a blank ChatGPT interface loading. Cut to a photo-style shot of an unfinished building complex.

VO: On November 30th, 2022, a company most people had never heard of quietly published a blog post. No launch event. No press conference. Just a free research preview called ChatGPT.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Just a blog post from OpenAI announcing the availability."
[Source: History.com]

VO: I was there that day. Not because I predicted anything. Because of a habit I'd built years before I had a name for it.

VISUAL: Title card. "EARLY TO BIG THINGS"

## Part 1: The story everyone tells (0:40-2:30)

VISUAL: A graph animating upward, sharply.

VO: Here's the number that made ChatGPT a legend. One million users in five days.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "A hundred million users within two months, a record at the time for any consumer application."
[Source: History.com]

VO: Two years later, a different kind of launch. A 90-day pop-up, in an unfinished building complex, for about a hundred people.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "A largely uncompleted complex" at Forest City, Johor.
[Source: Deeper Insights]

VO: That was Network School. I was there for that one too. This is the part where the story is supposed to turn into a lesson about foresight. About seeing the future before everyone else does. That story is wrong, and I want to explain why, because the real version is more useful.

## Part 2: The illusion (2:30-4:00)

VISUAL: A wall of small tiles, most of them dim, a few lit up.

VO: Here's what people don't see when they call it foresight. The actual hit rate.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Most of what you touch will be nothing. That is the cost, and it is cheap."
[Source: early-to-big-things.md]

VO: I've tried dozens of things that turned out to be nothing. Tools that died, communities that fizzled, ideas that were early in the wrong direction entirely. Nobody puts that reel together. You only ever hear about the two that worked.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Being early is not about predicting the future."
[Source: early-to-big-things.md]

VO: If it were prediction, I'd have a much higher hit rate. I don't. What I have is a method, and it's less impressive than foresight, and more repeatable.

## Part 3: The room where it happened (4:00-6:30)

VISUAL: Timeline graphic. 2020, 2022, 2024 marked in sequence.

VO: The method is this.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Lowering your own activation energy to try the new thing before it is comfortable."
[Source: early-to-big-things.md]

VO: I finished high school in 2020.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Finished high school in 2020."
[Source: adampang.com/about]

VO: Graduated App Academy as a software engineer in 2022. The same year that blog post went up.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Graduated from App Academy in 2022 as a software engineer."
[Source: adampang.com/about]

VO: I didn't wait for a review, or a friend's recommendation, or a headline confirming it mattered. I had the tools to go touch it that day, so I did. Two years later, when a hundred people were betting on an unfinished building in Malaysia, I was one of them.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Present at Network School's launch in 2024."
[Source: adampang.com/about]

VO: Neither of those moments felt historic while they were happening. ChatGPT was a research preview. Network School was a 90-day pilot that might have folded in a month. The activation energy wasn't "believe this will work." It was just "go find out."

## Part 4: What it compounds into (6:30-8:30)

VISUAL: A montage-style grid of small project logos or placeholder tiles.

VO: I moved to Network School full-time the following year.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "Moved to Network School full-time in 2025."
[Source: adampang.com/about]

VO: Became longtermer number two.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "I moved to Network School full-time and became longtermer number two."
[Source: pangaea.blog/posts/pangaea]

VO: Nine apps shipped since. Five live projects running at once.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "9 apps shipped... strummer.fun, pangaea.blog, pokedex.life, optimism.fun, interneta.world."
[Source: adampang.com/about]

VO: None of that compounds from one lucky guess. It compounds from a rate: how many times you were willing to go touch the new thing before it was comfortable, before it was validated, before anyone told you it was allowed to be exciting yet.

## Outro (8:30-9:00)

VISUAL: End card, plain text on paper background.

ON SCREEN QUOTE: "If you are early enough, often enough, people start to say you see the future. You do not. You just showed up on day one."
[Source: early-to-big-things.md]

VO: I don't see the future. I just show up on day one, as often as I can afford to be wrong.
