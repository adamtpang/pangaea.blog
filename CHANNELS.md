# Channels

Real name everywhere. Skin in the game. Separate accounts per lane, because the
audiences genuinely differ and the algorithms reward one subject per account.

**The rule an account has to pass before it exists:** a distinct audience, AND
enough supply to post weekly without inventing anything. An account that goes
quiet is worse than an account that was never opened, because it reads as
abandoned. Three fed accounts beat eight starved ones.

## Do NOT open these

- **A separate philosophy X.** Your tech audience IS your philosophy audience.
  Farcaster and X already reward you for mixing them. Splitting halves both.
- **A music X.** X is close to useless for music discovery. Put music where
  people go for music.
- **A business TikTok.** Wrong room. Your business work belongs on X and
  LinkedIn.
- **Threads, Bluesky, a newsletter that is not Substack.** Later, or never.

That leaves **four new accounts**: music IG, music TikTok, Spotify via a
distributor, and a private family IG. Everything else already exists.

## Spotify is not an account, it is a distributor

You cannot upload to Spotify directly. You pay a distributor and they place you
on Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok's library, YouTube Music, and the rest, then you
claim your Spotify for Artists profile.

- **DistroKid**, about $22 a year, unlimited releases. Right choice for someone
  shipping constantly.
- TuneCore and CD Baby charge per release, which is the wrong shape for 1,000
  songs.

Do this once and the entire back catalogue has somewhere to land. It also makes
`spotify:` in the songs schema real instead of aspirational.

## The engine: one session, one asset, three posts

The music practice IS the content. Do not create content separately.

1. **Film the session.** Phone on a stand, one angle, while you work a template.
2. **Cut 30 seconds** of the best moment. Vertical.
3. That single clip goes to **IG Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.** Same file,
   three posts, no extra work.
4. The finished track goes to **SoundCloud** now and **Spotify** at release.
5. The liner notes become a **post on pangaea.blog**, which feeds Substack and X.

One 45-minute Template A session produces: a song, three short videos, a blog
post, and an entry on the grid. That is the whole media stack from one sitting.

## Why the lanes stay separate but the person does not

Same name, same face, all of it linkable. The separation is for the algorithm
and for the viewer's expectation, not to hide anything. Someone who followed you
for guitar takes did not sign up for Delaware LLC posts, and the reverse. Both
accounts can say "Adam Pang" and link to pangaea.blog, which stays the one place
where the whole body of work sits together.

pangaea.blog is the hub. The accounts are spokes. Publish here, syndicate there.
