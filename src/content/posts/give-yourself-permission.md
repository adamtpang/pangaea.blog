Nobody is coming to tap you on the shoulder and declare you allowed. There is no ceremony, no email, no committee that reviews your case and grants you clearance to start. The permission you are waiting for from outside is a permission only you can issue, and you have been able to issue it the entire time.

This sounds obvious and changes nothing when heard as an idea. It only works as an instruction: today, pick the thing you have been waiting to be allowed to do, and do the smallest real version of it, on purpose, as an act of self-permission rather than an accident.

Waiting for permission is comfortable because it is not your fault yet. Giving it to yourself removes that cover completely, which is exactly why it works.
