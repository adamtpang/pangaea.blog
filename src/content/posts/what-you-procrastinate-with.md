Watch what you do instead of the thing you're supposed to be doing. Not the mindless scrolling, the other thing: the side project you keep sneaking into, the book you read instead of the assignment, the conversation you have instead of the errand. That is not procrastination. That is your actual priority, wearing a disguise because you haven't given it permission to be the main thing yet.

Real procrastination has no direction, it just avoids. But most of what people call procrastination has a very clear direction, straight toward something they want more and haven't admitted wanting.

So audit your avoidance. Whatever you reach for when you're supposed to be doing something else is rarely random. It's usually the truth, arriving early, before you were ready to hear it.
