Get to the group chat as fast as you possibly can. That's the actual instruction, not "post more" or "grow your audience." Public platforms are the waiting room. You post, you reply, you build a small reputation in full view of strangers, and none of it means you're actually inside anything yet. The real relationships, the ones that produce collaborations and help when you need it, moved into group chats years ago, and they don't show up on any profile.

Getting into the right group chat is a different achievement than getting a lot of followers, and it runs on a completely different audience magnitude: ten people who actually read what you send versus ten thousand who scrolled past it. Nobody invites a stranger with a big audience into a small private thread. They invite the person who was consistently useful, generous, and specific in public first, because the public behavior is the audition for the private room.

So the actual goal of posting in public was never the post. It's the invitation that eventually shows up in your DMs. Treat every public interaction as a tryout for a room you can't see yet, and stop mistaking the waiting room for the destination. See [[Audience Magnitudes]] for why the small room, once you're in it, moves faster than the big one ever could.
