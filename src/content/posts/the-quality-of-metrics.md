We measure what can be counted, not what matters, and then quietly start treating the count as the thing itself. Views, followers, revenue, streaks: all of it quantity, standing in for quality because quality refuses to sit still long enough to be numbered.

This isn't a scandal, it's just physics. A number is legible from across the room. A good sentence, a right decision, a life well spent, none of that transmits at a glance. So the dashboard fills with what's countable, and the eye follows the dashboard.

The fix isn't rejecting metrics. It's remembering, on purpose, that every number you're staring at is a shadow of the actual thing, and shadows are easy to optimize while the thing itself walks away.
