AI doesn't have taste, opinions, or a self it's protecting. It has whatever you put in, computed further. Ask it something lazy, get something lazy back, dressed in confident sentences. Ask it something sharp, get something sharp. The model isn't the ceiling, the prompt is.

This is uncomfortable for exactly the people who most need AI, because a mirror shows you your own resolution. A vague thinker gets vague output at inhuman speed. A precise thinker gets precise output at inhuman speed. The gap between them, which used to be hidden by how slow both were, is suddenly visible and enormous.

So the actual skill AI rewards isn't prompting. It's knowing what you want clearly enough to ask for it. Everyone got a mirror with compute attached. Most people are still checking if it's plugged in.
