Ten people who actually get what you're building beat a thousand who scrolled past it. The randos inflate a number. The ten tell you if the thing works.

Chase the number and you'll optimize for whatever travels fastest, which is rarely the same as whatever is good. Chase the ten and you'll optimize for depth: would this specific person, who I respect, actually use this, actually feel something, actually tell a friend.

Audience size is a vanity metric wearing a business metric's costume. Audience quality is the real one. Find your ten first. The thousand, if they ever show up, are downstream.
