Most of what separates someone who makes things from someone who doesn't isn't skill. It's the settled, boring knowledge that they can, in fact, sit down and make something, because they have done it before and the world did not end.

That knowledge takes exactly one method to build: doing the thing badly, repeatedly, until "can I actually do this" stops being a live question. After that, the work is just work. Before that, every session is also a referendum on your identity, which is exhausting and has nothing to do with the actual craft.

So the fastest way to become an artist is not a better technique. It's enough finished, mediocre reps that the question closes for good.
