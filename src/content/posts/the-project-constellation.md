For years I couldn't explain the pattern across what I was building. Wonderhall is a concert series, a room on a schedule where people hear the same thing together. Summon is a way to talk to the people who shaped you, the ones you learned from without ever being in a room with them. Optimism maps what humanity actually wants, onto one surface, so it can be seen at once. Pangaea is a blog named after the moment before the land broke apart.

Looked at individually, they read like scattered interests. Looked at together, they're the same object built from different materials, over and over: a room, on purpose, for something scattered to gather in. I didn't plan the pattern. I just kept building it with whatever material was in front of me, which is usually what happens when something unfinished in you keeps finding new ways to say itself.

That's the real use of a constellation over a single star: no one project has to carry the whole thesis alone. Each one is a data point, and the shape only becomes visible once you stop asking what any single project is for and start asking what all of them, together, keep insisting on.
