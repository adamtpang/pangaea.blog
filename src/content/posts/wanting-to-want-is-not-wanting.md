There's a difference between wanting a thing and wanting to want it, and most stalled ambition lives entirely in the second category. You don't actually want to run the marathon. You want to be the kind of person who wants to run marathons, which sounds identical from the inside and produces exactly zero training runs.

Real wanting pulls you toward action without needing a pep talk. Wanting to want just produces guilt, because you keep failing to feel a desire that was never really there, mistaking the absence of desire for a failure of discipline.

So when something stays permanently on the list, ask which one it is. If it's the second kind, stop forcing it and let it go. You cannot discipline your way into wanting something. You can only find what you actually want, underneath the thing you thought you should.
