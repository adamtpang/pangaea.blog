If I had a year left to live, I would write more and love more. I know this without needing the diagnosis to confirm it, which means the honest move is to just do it now, before any doctor makes it official. The deadline was never the missing ingredient. Permission was.

Writing especially, because writing is setting the truth free. Most of what you actually think stays locked up, half formed, revised into politeness before it ever reaches another person, or reaches paper at all. A page a day is where the unedited version gets to exist. Not the version calibrated for an audience, the one that was true before you started managing how it sounded.

That's the part worth sitting with. If the answer to "what would you do with a year left" is available to you right now, you don't need urgency to be real to act like it is. You just need to start believing you might not finish the sentence, and write the true one anyway.
