The mind likes to think it runs the show, issuing verdicts about mood and meaning from some throne above the body. Mostly it's lying. Sleep four hours and watch how philosophical your problems suddenly become. Skip meals and see how your patience, a supposedly moral trait, evaporates on schedule.

Mental health gets treated as a separate department from physical health, something to solve with willpower or therapy or reading the right book. Often it is closer to plumbing. Fix the sleep, the food, the movement, the sunlight, and half the "psychological" problem quietly resolves, because it was never purely psychological to begin with.

This isn't a claim that the mind doesn't matter. It's a claim about where to look first when something feels wrong and won't explain itself. Ask the body before you ask the story. The body votes first, and the mind mostly just counts the ballots.
