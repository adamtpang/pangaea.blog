Popularity measures reach: how many people saw it. Quality measures whether it was good. These get confused constantly because the popular thing is usually in front of you and the good thing usually isn't, so popularity becomes the only signal available and gets treated as proof.

Time spent is the more sophisticated version of the same mistake. Engagement, watch time, scroll depth: better than a raw view count, but still a proxy. A slot machine holds your attention for hours and improves nothing about your life. Attention is a measure of grip, not of worth.

Neither number tells you if the thing was actually good. Only asking "did this make anything better" does, and that question doesn't show up on any dashboard.
