The dog doesn't care about your calendar. It needs the walk regardless of the deadline, the mood, the weather, and it has no interest in your reasons for skipping it. That's the whole use of a dog you wouldn't get from a note that says "go outside": it can't be negotiated with.

So the walk happens, and only afterward do you notice you got the thing you actually needed and had been putting off giving yourself. Air, movement, a change of room, twenty minutes where the only job is watching an animal sniff a hedge.

You were never really walking the dog. You were using the dog as cover to walk yourself, because "I need a break" doesn't get you off the couch but "the dog needs a walk" does. Keep the dog. Or invent an equivalent excuse that can't say no to itself.
