The internet doesn't just carry information, it changes shape based on what moves through it, the same way a brain thickens the paths it uses and lets the unused ones fade. A meme that gets repeated enough stops being a joke and becomes infrastructure. A framing that gets used enough stops being an opinion and becomes the default lens millions of people reach for without noticing they're reaching.

This means the internet has something like plasticity, and plasticity means direction matters. Every repost, every quote, every algorithm nudge is a tiny synaptic strengthening, voting on which patterns get to become permanent.

You are not just a user of this brain. You are one of the signals rewiring it. What you amplify today is closer to infrastructure tomorrow than you'd like to admit.
