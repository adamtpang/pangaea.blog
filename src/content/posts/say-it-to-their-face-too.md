Talking well behind someone's back is not a substitute for saying it to their face. It is the proof that the face version was true. If you would not repeat the compliment with them in the room, it was never really praise, it was just something nice sounding you said to someone else.

The two have to travel together. Praise only behind their back can curdle into something else, a story you tell about a person rather than to them, and stories about people drift from the truth faster than you'd like.

So say the good thing twice. Once to the room that will carry it back to them eventually. Once directly, now, while it still costs you something to say it. The version that costs you something is the one that counts.
