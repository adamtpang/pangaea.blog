The thing you are chasing is not sitting still waiting to be found. It is also moving, also looking, in whatever way a goal, a craft, or a calling can be said to look: gathering the people and conditions that would let it exist, same as you are gathering the skill and courage that would let you reach it.

That is not mysticism. It is just what happens when two things are actually suited to each other: the useful skill starts finding the people who need it, and the person who needs it starts finding the skill, and the meeting point arrives from both directions at once, faster than either side moving alone.

So keep moving toward what you want. It was never only your job to close the distance.
