One person you actually admire watching the whole thing, and telling you what worked, is worth more than a hundred thousand strangers scrolling past for two seconds. Not metaphorically. Actually more useful, more motivating, more likely to make the next thing better.

The strangers give you a number. The hero gives you a signal, calibrated by someone whose judgment you already trust, which is the only kind of feedback dense enough to actually change what you do next.

So stop building for the crowd you'll never meet. Build for the five or ten people whose opinion would genuinely wreck or make your day, and let the crowd be a side effect.
