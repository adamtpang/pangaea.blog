Some music sounds like the parts of a person that don't get invited to dinner: the disgust, the panic, the joke that's too dark to tell out loud. That is not a flaw in it. That is the whole function. A shadow, in the psychological sense, is everything you've disowned because it didn't fit the story you tell about yourself, and it does not go away from being disowned. It just runs the show from underneath.

Music that lets the shadow speak directly, without cleaning it up for polite company, does something a well behaved song cannot: it gives the disowned part somewhere to go that isn't acting out in your actual life. You feel the chaos on purpose, in a controlled room, and it costs you nothing but forty minutes.

That is worth more than comfort music. Comfort music agrees with the story you already tell about yourself. Shadow music argues with it, and the argument is usually the more honest of the two.
