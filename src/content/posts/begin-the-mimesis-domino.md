People copy what they see modeled, not what they're told is good. You are far more likely to start a project because you watched a friend start one than because someone gave you a lecture on starting projects. Wanting is contagious before it is rational.

Which means the fastest way to cause more of something, more art, more building, more honesty, is not to argue for it. It's to do it visibly, first, so someone nearby catches the wanting off you the way you caught it off someone else.

You didn't invent your ambitions from nothing. Somebody's domino hit yours. Set up the next one and knock it over. You won't get to watch every tile fall, but that was never really the point of dominoes.
