The version of you that only exists when no one is watching is the real one. Everything performed for an audience, even an audience of one person you're trying to impress, gets edited before it leaves you. Privacy is the only room where the editing stops.

This is why the truest thing about someone often comes out in what they do alone: what they read with no one checking, what they build with no plan to ship it, what they say to themselves in the car. Not because the public version is fake, but because it has an audience baked into its shape, and shape changes under an audience.

Guard your private hours the way you'd guard a witness. They are the only ones who saw you when you weren't performing being you.
