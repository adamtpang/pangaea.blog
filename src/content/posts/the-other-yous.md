Somewhere online there are people running a version of your exact interior monologue, the specific combination of obsessions that feels too idiosyncratic to be shared by anyone. The old world gave you the people in your building. The internet gives you a search across every mind that's ever typed anything, tuned to find the ones running close to your frequency.

That is what following someone, really, is: not fandom, a resonance test. You read a stranger's words and some part of you goes oh, you too, and the search narrows a little.

You are not looking for people like you in general. You are looking for the other yous, the ones one decision away from being you, so you can borrow what they already learned.
