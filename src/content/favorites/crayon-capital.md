What I actually want from this channel is the topic instinct, not the production style. A famous name, a simple story, told properly: that's the whole trick. You click because you recognize "Rockefeller" or "1929," you stay because it teaches you something real about a thing you thought you already understood.

I'd already written this down once before I could articulate it: a stray capture from August 12th says "create my own crayon capital using google docs plans," logged and parked the same day next to a kpop suite idea and an energy-tooling suite idea, in a batch that got flagged as the exact scatter problem I'm trying to fix (new-idea-a-minute instead of finishing what's already started).

So the honest version isn't "build a Crayon Capital clone." It's: take the one real principle (recognizable name, real research, simple story) and use it to pick topics for the mini-films already in `reels/`, where the sourcing discipline was already stricter than the original channel's own shortcuts.
