Two different files track two different things I take from him. `WRITERS-STUDIED.md` has the craft: how his sentences actually work, the rhythm, the specificity. `FRIENDLY-AMBITIOUS-NERD.md` has the philosophy: his own guide to friendship, ambition, and taking your own curiosity seriously, distilled from his book of the same name.

He's the reference point for what it looks like to just be prolific, publicly, imperfectly, for years, and have that turn into an actual body of work and an actual life. That's the whole bet behind Pangaea too.
