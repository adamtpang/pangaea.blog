# The 1,000 video essays

## The insight that makes 1,000 possible

**Video is not a sixth thing to make. It is a lens on the five you already
make.** Every song session, every app build, every essay, every study is
already a video; it just goes unfilmed.

You do not need 1,000 video ideas. You need a camera running while you do the
work that is already scheduled. The queue below is derived from the other
pillars, not invented alongside them.

## The model: Van Neistat

He calls it the **industrial essay film**. The structure is the useful part:

> One 30-minute flagship each month, built from rotating segments (The Build,
> A Thing We Love, Ranked List, Fast Mock). Over the following weeks, each
> segment is released again as its own standalone video.

So one shoot produces **one flagship plus five to seven standalone videos.**
That is the whole leverage. At one flagship a month you are publishing weekly
without ever having a second idea.

What makes his channel work is not production value. It is a **repeatable
format plus a strong point of view**, handmade rather than glossy, repair
rather than replace. He famously does not watch YouTube, which is why it does
not look like YouTube.

## Your segment library

Six recurring formats, each drawn from work you already do. The `format:` field
in the schema takes these.

| Format | What it is | Fed by | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| **build** | an app going from nothing to live | 46 apps | 6 to 10 min |
| **take** | one song session, start to bounce | the songs queue | 4 to 8 min |
| **study** | pull a song apart with stem separation, then steal from it | SONGWRITERS.md | 8 to 12 min |
| **ranked** | a ranked list, argued, with evidence | the indexes | 10 to 15 min |
| **thing** | one object or tool you love, examined closely | life | 3 to 5 min |
| **field** | somewhere you actually are: NS, Batam, Guam | travel | 5 to 10 min |
| **flagship** | monthly, all of the above stitched together | the month | 25 to 35 min |

## The math to 1,000

You already have the supply. It has simply never been filmed.

| Source | Videos available |
|---|---|
| Songs (959 queued starting positions) | one **take** each |
| Apps (46 live, more coming) | one **build** each |
| Studies (16 queued, extensible) | one **study** each |
| Essays (197 files) | the strongest become **ranked** or **thing** |
| Flagships | 12 a year, each stitched from the above |

The bottleneck is not ideas. It is pressing record.

## The rule

**Film the session you were going to do anyway.** Never schedule a shoot.
A shoot is an event, and events get postponed; a running camera during work you
already committed to costs nothing extra and cannot be procrastinated.

Phone on a stand, one angle, no lighting setup. If the take is good, you have a
video. If it is not, you still made the song.

## The cut

Free the whole way, on hardware you own:

1. **DaVinci Resolve** (free) for the edit. Your RTX 3070 handles it easily.
2. **Whisper** locally for transcripts and captions, same GPU that runs demucs.
3. **Auphonic** (2 free hours a month) for audio polish.

That is the Descript workflow at zero cost, which matters now that the credits
ran out.

## The multiplier

Every video yields four assets:

1. the **long cut** for YouTube
2. one **30-second vertical** for Reels, TikTok, Shorts (same file, three posts)
3. the **transcript**, which becomes a post on pangaea.blog
4. the **artifact itself**: the song, the app, the study

One session, one camera, four pillars fed.

## Numbering

`vlog:` is the odometer toward 1,000, the way `number:` works for songs and
essays. `covers:` points at the song or app the video documents, so the graph
can show which pillars feed which.
